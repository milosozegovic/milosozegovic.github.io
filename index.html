About

C and Pascal at school didn't take. PHP did. I took a course at 17, was working as a developer within ten months, and left university at 20 because I wanted the real thing.

Before any of that, my final school project was a robotic arm — assembled from a kit and programmed in C to pick up cubes and put them somewhere specific.

That's still roughly what I do: take something that works badly and find out why. These days it's usually a query. Sometimes it's a motorcycle, a sawmill control panel, or a desk.
